F3 question 3 -- what "only on the direct path" can mean when there are several

Three readings, measured on the same fixture:

  union      every container that is an ancestor of some fact the principal may read
  anchored   that set, intersected with the principal's own accessible set
  intersect  per fact, the containers on EVERY path upward from it

`leak` counts containers the union reading makes list-visible that the principal
holds no grant on and none below -- containers whose existence it learns from a
grant somewhere else. kind 0 tenant, 1 graph, 2 episode, 3 entity-set.
